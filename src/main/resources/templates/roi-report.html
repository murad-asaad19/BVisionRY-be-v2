A founder’s score is the overall readiness score of that assessment — the same number they see on their own results. The pillar table is the paired, per-pillar view of those same assessments, so a founder’s change and a pillar’s change need not match. A dash means the measurement does not exist: a founder assessed once has an intake score and no change, and a founder never assessed has neither. Nothing on this page is estimated.

How to read this report

Every score is a founder’s evaluated assessment on a 0–100 scale. Intake is a founder’s first evaluated assessment in this program; latest is their most recent. Pillar averages compare the same founders at both points, so a change reflects the founders themselves — not a change in who was measured.

Module completion counts every program step assigned to each founder against the ones they submitted, including steps whose module has not been released to them yet.
